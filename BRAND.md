# Brand & Messaging Guidelines

## Approved positioning
- “Community Support Token”
- “Meme/Fan token”
- “Cultural participation”
- “Transparent community coordination”
- “No ownership. No profit rights. No promised returns.”

## Disallowed positioning (never use)
- “Investment”, “returns”, “ROI”, “profit”
- “Guaranteed”, “safe”, “risk-free”
- “We will pump”, “we will raise the price”
- “Treasury yield”, “APY”, “staking returns”
- “Buybacks”, “price support”, “floor price”
- “Government-backed”, “SEC-approved”, “White House aligned”

## Non-endorsement requirement
All public pages and token descriptions should include:
“Not affiliated with or endorsed by the SEC, the White House, or any government agency.”

## Tone
- Clear, calm, and compliance-first.
- Avoid hype.
- Focus on culture + community outputs.
