# Risk Disclosure

Digital assets are risky. Key risks include (not exhaustive):
- **Volatility:** price can move sharply and unpredictably.
- **Liquidity risk:** you may not be able to sell when you want.
- **Smart contract / platform risk:** bugs, outages, or changes in platform behavior.
- **Regulatory uncertainty:** laws and guidance can change.
- **Scams & impersonators:** always verify official links and announcements.

Participation should be considered optional cultural participation. Do not spend money you cannot afford to lose.
