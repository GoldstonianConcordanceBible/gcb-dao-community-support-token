# FAQ

## Is this an investment?
No. This is a meme/fan token for cultural participation and coordination. No ownership. No profit rights. No promised returns.

## Is it “SEC approved” or “White House aligned”?
No. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency. We use a compliance-first approach and avoid security-like features.

## Is this a donation or “tax deductible tithe”?
No. Token purchases are not presented as donations or tax deductible. (If you want donation language, use proper charitable structures and separate rails.)

## What does holding the token do?
It may grant non-financial community features like roles/badges and voting on non-financial priorities.

## Will the team try to raise the price?
No. We do not market or operate this as a “number-go-up” product.
