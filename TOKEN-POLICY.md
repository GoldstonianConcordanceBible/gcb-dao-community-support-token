# Token Policy (Hard Rules)

This token is designed and communicated as a meme/fan token for cultural participation.

## Non-negotiables
- No ownership, equity, or profit rights
- No dividends, revenue share, or treasury distributions to holders
- No “staking returns”, APY, yield, or similar
- No buyback promises or “price support”
- No marketing that suggests profit or appreciation

## Utility scope (allowed)
- Roles / badges / recognition
- Voting on non-financial community priorities
- Access to free community resources

## Compliance-first communications
All official descriptions must include the disclaimer language in DISCLAIMER.md.
