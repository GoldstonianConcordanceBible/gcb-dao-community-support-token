# The Goldstonian Concordance Bible DAO — Community Support Token (CST)

## Core positioning (short)
**Community Support Token:** a meme/fan token for cultural participation and transparent community coordination around the GCB ecosystem. **No ownership. No profit rights. No promised returns.**

## What this is
GCB CST is a community meme/fan token used for:
- Identity & cultural participation
- Transparent coordination (public proposals, budgets, and deliverables)
- Community recognition (badges/roles)
- Prioritizing non-financial community outputs (study guides, printables, events)

## What this is NOT
This token is **not**:
- equity, a share, an investment product, or a “tithing security”
- a promise of profit, yield, dividends, buybacks, or price support
- a claim on assets, revenue, or any treasury distributions to holders
