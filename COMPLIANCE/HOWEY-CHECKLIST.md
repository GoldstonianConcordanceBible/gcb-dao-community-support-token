# Howey / Investment-Contract Red Flags Checklist

If any are true, STOP and revise messaging/features:

- Are we implying purchasers should expect profits?
- Are we promising “returns”, “yield”, “APY”, “revenue share”, “dividends”?
- Are we saying the team will increase token value?
- Are we doing buybacks or liquidity support?
- Are holders entitled to treasury distributions?

If “yes” to any: remove the feature/language and update docs.
