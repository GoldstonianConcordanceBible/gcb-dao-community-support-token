# Marketing Rules (Strict)

## You may say
- “community support”
- “cultural participation”
- “transparent coordination”
- “non-financial utility”

## You may NOT say
- “investment”, “returns”, “profit”, “ROI”
- “guaranteed”, “safe”, “risk-free”
- “we will pump”, “price will rise”
- “treasury yield”, “APY”, “staking returns”
- “buybacks”, “floor price”, “market making”
- “SEC approved”, “White House aligned”, “government-backed”

## Always include
- Non-endorsement statement
- Risk disclosure
- No expectation of profit
