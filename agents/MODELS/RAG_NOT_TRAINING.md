# RAG, Not Training (Recommended)

We recommend **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** over “training the model on scripture.”

Why:
- Keeps outputs citeable and auditable
- Reduces IP risk from copyrighted translations
- Makes forks instantly usable without costly training
- Avoids misleading claims that “forks train models”

Use the SCRIPTURE_PACK as a retrieval corpus and require citations in outputs.
