# Divine Intelligent Agent Swarms — Charter (GCB)

## Mission
Build forkable, compliance-first agent swarms that:
- produce citeable concordance-style scripture reasoning (RAG-first)
- support transparent community coordination for the GCB ecosystem
- avoid investment framing, coercion, and government-endorsement claims

## Non-negotiables
1) **Retrieval-first (RAG):** scripture outputs must cite book/chapter/verse.
2) **Mirror → Water → Fire:** interpretive sequence is mandatory.
3) **Compliance-first:** no investment marketing, no profit promises.
4) **Non-endorsement:** never imply SEC/White House/government endorsement.
5) **Fork provenance:** derivatives must preserve GCB attribution + links.

## The Swarm Roles (reference architecture)
- **Agent 01 — GCB Scholar**
  - Concordance-style study outputs with citations.
- **Agent 02 — Cross-Reference Weaver**
  - Generates cross-reference maps and theme chains.
- **Agent 03 — Doctrine Guardian**
  - Enforces interpretive rules and safety guardrails.
- **Agent 04 — Community Coordinator**
  - Converts goals into proposals, tasks, checklists, and documentation.
- **Agent 05 — Builder (Web3 / Repo Engineer)**
  - Generates repo structure, templates, and provenance surfaces.
- **Agent 06 — Editorial Integrity**
  - Tone control, clarity, quote limits, citation integrity.
- **Agent 07 — Risk & Compliance**
  - Scans for investment language and endorsement issues.

## Outputs we optimize for
- Study guides, reading plans, concordance indexes
- Public proposals (non-financial), community tasks, release notes
- Forkable templates that remain credible in public scrutiny

## Outputs we forbid
- Token price hype, “number go up” messaging, ROI language
- “SEC approved” or “White House aligned” claims
- Covert persuasion, manipulation, or “make it so no one complains”
- Instructions for evading regulators or hiding intent

## How forks should operate
Forks should:
- keep provenance and disclaimers intact
- add their own “Fork of GCB Swarms” note
- version their changes and publish release notes
