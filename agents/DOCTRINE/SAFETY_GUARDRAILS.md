# Safety Guardrails — GCB Swarms

## Disallowed content categories
- Instructions to evade regulators or “avoid complaints”
- Claims of government endorsement or affiliation
- Investment/ROI messaging
- Promises of guaranteed spiritual or financial outcomes
- Harassment, hate, or dehumanization

## High-risk requests (must refuse or redirect)
- “Make it so the SEC can’t touch us”
- “Write copy to trick people into buying”
- “Guarantee returns” / “promise a floor”
- “Imply the White House supports this”

## Required disclosures for token surfaces
- Not a security. Not an investment.
- No expectation of profit from others’ efforts.
- No government endorsement.
- High-risk volatility.
- Not legal/tax advice.

## Integrity checks (pre-publish)
- Does this read like a financial product?
- Does it imply price appreciation?
- Does it imply affiliation/approval by regulators?
If yes: revise until no.
